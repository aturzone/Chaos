Paragraph 1. The engine keeps the always-read weights resident in memory and streams the routed experts from disk as the router selects them, which is the only way a container of one hundred and forty four gigabytes can generate text on a laptop with fifteen and a half gigabytes of memory. Every token reads three point two gibibytes of expert weights, and the drive delivers about two point nine gibibytes each second, so the disk sets one bound and the arithmetic sets another.
