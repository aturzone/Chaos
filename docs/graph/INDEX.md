- research/step-count-never-settles-2026-08-24.md — **the one lever left after "small images are the model" turns out to change the picture without ever converging, so there is no step count that is correct.** Two instruments and one of them misused. **Reconstruction** (noise a known latent at σ=0.6, integrate back): error 0.3683 at 4 steps against 0.3770 at 50 — flat, slightly backwards, and **50 steps costs 17x what 4 does**.
